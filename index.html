Our Core Program

Thabaat

ثبات · steadfastness, firmness of heart

Nobody should walk into Islam alone.

Thabaat is a national support program for new Muslims, built jointly by M1U Foundation and the North American Imams Fellow. It pairs every new Muslim with a trained, screened companion within 48 hours of their shahada — and stays with them for a full year.

We built a front door.
Nobody staffed the back door.

A person takes their shahada. The masjid erupts in takbir. Forty people embrace them, someone presses a phone number into their hand, and they go home.

Within a month, the numbers have gone cold. Family has stopped speaking to them. Friends have drifted. The community that celebrated them has moved on to the next celebration.

What follows is not a crisis of belief. It is a crisis of belonging — and no one is assigned to catch it.

Net zero Islam in the U.S. gains roughly as many converts as it loses, so conversion has little net effect on the size of the American Muslim population.

Rafeeq
Companionship

A screened, trained companion who walks alongside a new Muslim for twelve months. Rafeeq means companion, not mentor — the difference is the point.

Ta'leem
Formation

A twelve-week Foundations cohort built around daily practice and lived transition — prayer, family, halal living, workplace — rather than abstract doctrine.

Sanad
Support

Scholars for direct questions, gender-specific peer circles, licensed counseling by referral, and a staffed national line that a real person answers.

Safeguarding is not a footnote

New Muslims are often at their most isolated and least protected in the months after shahada. Every commitment below is in place before a single participant is enrolled.

✓National background check and registry search on every companion, repeated every two years
✓Same-gender pairing as the default for all one-to-one contact
✓Sixteen-hour certification with a pass requirement and annual recertification
✓Published code of conduct barring romantic pursuit and private financial arrangements
✓Mental health care delivered only by licensed clinicians — never by volunteers
✓Participant privacy protected by role. No public directory, ever.
✓Supervision at one supervisor per twelve companions. Nobody works unsupervised.
✓A complaint pathway that never routes through the person being complained about

The Rafeeq Corps

Rafeeqs are screened, certified, and supervised volunteers who commit to twelve months with one person. Recruitment opens alongside the first pilot cohort, and every applicant goes through references, a background check, a structured interview, and sixteen hours of certification before they are matched with anyone.

For imams and masjids

The intake protocol, curriculum, and companion certification are offered at no cost to imams and to existing new-Muslim support programs. Where a capable program already exists, Thabaat equips it rather than competing with it.

Thabaat is in development.
The first pilot cohort launches in 2026. Site announcements and Rafeeq recruitment details to follow.

The Team Behind the Mission

Dr. Abdulhakim Mohamed

President, M1U Foundation · CEO, North American Imams Fellow

Dr. Mohamed has dedicated his professional life to the service and advancement of Muslim religious leaders in North America. He is a founder of the North American Imams Fellow, where he serves as Chief Executive Officer, and leads M1U Foundation as its President.

NAIF was built on the conviction that imams deserve the same professional infrastructure as any other learned profession — and that communities are stronger when their religious leaders are properly supported. Under his leadership it has grown into a national community of more than 900 imams, with regional chapters, an annual convention, and professional development programs reaching religious leaders across the United States and Canada.

At M1U, that same conviction shapes the work: build the infrastructure the community is missing, and support the people already doing the work rather than competing with them.

How M1U Operates

M1U Foundation Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Our programs are governed by a board, and our operating costs are covered separately through partnerships, sponsorships, and organizational support.

We would rather run three programs properly than ten thinly. Where a capable organization already does the work, we support it instead of duplicating it.

"Most nonprofits ask: will you donate?
M1U asks a different question:
Will you help build a stronger Ummah?"
🛡️

Safeguarding first

Background checks, supervision, and a published code of conduct are in place before any program touches a participant.

🔒

Privacy protected

Participant information is restricted by role and never published. For many people we serve, privacy is a matter of real-world safety.

📊

Measured honestly

We report what actually happened — including what didn't work. Activity is not the same as outcome, and we don't confuse the two.

🤲

Trust is an amanah

Every relationship, every referral, and every rupiah of support is treated as a trust we are answerable for.
