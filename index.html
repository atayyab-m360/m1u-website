Our Mission
M1U strengthens the imams, masajid, and waqf of the ummah so that every community can receive those coming to Islam, serve those living it, and endure beyond those who built it.

Waqf is an Islamic endowment. An asset is dedicated permanently and can never be sold or inherited; only what it generates is spent. It is how institutions outlast the people who build them.

M1U Foundation Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit serving as a bridge between people, masajid, nonprofits, and businesses, creating partnerships that multiply impact instead of dividing it.

M1U Initiative

Thabaat

In Development
ثبات · steadfastness, firmness of heart

Nobody should walk into Islam alone.

Thabaat is M1U’s structured support initiative for new Muslims and reverts. The program is being developed around a 12-week Foundations course, trained Rafeeq companions, intake and referral processes, matching, and ongoing follow-up. Program details and enrollment information will be published after the operating process is finalized.

Why "revert"? Every child is born upon the fitrah and is turned toward another faith by upbringing. Someone entering Islam is returning to an original state rather than adopting a foreign one.

We built a front door.
Nobody staffed the back door.

A person takes their shahada. The masjid erupts in takbir. Forty people embrace them, someone presses a phone number into their hand, and they go home.

Within a month, the numbers have gone cold. Family has stopped speaking to them. Friends have drifted. The community that celebrated them has moved on to the next celebration.

What follows is not a crisis of belief. It is a crisis of belonging. No one is assigned to catch it.

What is being developed

In Development
Foundations

A 12-week Foundations course focused on practical learning and lived transition is being developed.

In Development
Rafeeq

A training and certification framework for volunteer companions is being developed.

In Development
Support Process

Intake, referral, matching, case review, and follow-up procedures are being designed.

Thabaat is designed to support the work already being done by imams and masajid, not compete with it.

Our People

The Team Behind the Mission

Dr. Abdulhakim Mohamed

President, M1U Foundation Inc.

Dr. Abdulhakim Mohamed is President of M1U Foundation Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to advancing charitable and community-focused initiatives. He also serves as President of the Global Imams and Scholars Council and is a member of the Muslim World League.

Dr. Mohamed holds a Ph.D. in Islamic Studies and has served as an imam for over forty years. His career has also included work in education as both a teacher and a principal. He has been actively engaged in da'wah in the United States since 1995, dedicating his life to Islamic scholarship, community leadership, and service.

He is based in Michigan.

Leadership Team Day-to-day operations and program delivery

Abdul Tayyab

Vice President of Operations

Abdul Tayyab is an entrepreneur, operations strategist, and technology leader with a background in nonprofit and healthcare operations. As Vice President of Operations for M1U, he focuses on building scalable systems and supporting national initiatives.

He serves as Director of Customer Success for QubaLink/Minar360, working with masajid and community organizations to strengthen engagement through technology, and sits on the Executive Committee of OPEN DC.

Saba Asad

Secretary

Saba Asad is a marketing and community engagement leader with over fourteen years of experience in digital strategy, branding, technology, and nonprofit communications. She serves as Marketing Director at QubaLink and on the Board of Trustees of the ADAMS Center.

For over fifteen years she has been actively involved in volunteering and serving the Muslim community, supporting initiatives in education, outreach, communications, and organizational development. Her work is driven by a commitment to strengthening Muslim institutions and helping community initiatives grow from ideas into sustainable impact.

Board of Directors Governance and fiduciary oversight, distinct from day-to-day operations

Ahmed R. Ali

Founder & Chairman of the Board

Ahmed R. Ali is a US-based businessman, entrepreneur, investor, and philanthropist whose work is shaped by a belief that communities are strengthened not by individuals alone, but by institutions, shared responsibility, and people willing to build for generations they may never meet.

His philanthropic focus is on helping Muslim communities in North America become more organized, resilient, and institutionally prepared, particularly where access to leadership development, educational support, and sustainable funding is limited. For Ahmed, philanthropy is not only about giving; it is about helping communities stand with dignity, serve with excellence, and endure with purpose.

Our Commitment

How M1U Operates

M1U Foundation Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Our programs are governed by a board, and our operating costs are covered separately through partnerships, sponsorships, and organizational support.

We develop programs carefully, confirm community needs, and expand when the structure and support are ready. Where a capable organization already does the work, we support it instead of duplicating it.

"Most nonprofits ask: will you donate?
M1U asks a different question:
Will you help build a stronger Ummah?"
🛡️

Safeguarding first

Background checks, supervision, and a published code of conduct are in place before any program touches a participant.

🔒

Privacy protected

Participant information is restricted by role and never published. For many people we serve, privacy is a matter of real-world safety.

📊

Measured honestly

We report what actually happened, including what didn't work. Activity is not the same as outcome, and we don't confuse the two.

🤲

Trust is an amanah

Every relationship, every referral, and every dollar of support is treated as a trust we are answerable for.
